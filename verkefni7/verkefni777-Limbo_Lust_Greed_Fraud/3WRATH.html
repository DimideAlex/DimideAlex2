back

WRATH is the 5th layer of hell and is home to the oceon styx. If you read the text advertising WRATH, or you know something about dante's inferno, you should know that the layer was once home to the river styx but due to human history it has turned into the oceon styx, making it rather hard to traverse through the layer. With the soul exception of the Ferry Men of course, as the reason it isn't hard for the Ferry Men is because they use a gigantic cruise ship to traverse through out the layer. Said cruise ship is at least one third the size of the titanic so there is a lot of space for passengers and restaurants.

There is almost no good parts about WRATH except for the giant cruise ship. Atleast the ship is actually pretty nice as it has many floors and there for many rooms, there also a large cafeteria which has large veriety of food, and last but not least is the art in the ship as they are rather beuatiful and well made.

And of course just like the last two, WRATH has the all the things you need like: Shelter, food and water. There aren't that many residents though.

Similar to the last two, WRATH has a few flaws, and they are really important too so you better read this.

  1. The least of the problems is sea sickness, obvious enough. If you're sea sick then i recommend you not to go to the cruise.
  2. The other slight problem is that you need to have a full on 24karot gold coin to give to the Ferry Men.
  3. If you're scared of big fish then you'd be getting a heart attack from the Leviathan which isn't even a fish.
  4. And last but not least, if you ever piss off the Ferryman/men you might or will get smighted by lightning... Like actually you will.

I don't really know what i can say except for the fact that WRATH is a little dangerous. That's it.
