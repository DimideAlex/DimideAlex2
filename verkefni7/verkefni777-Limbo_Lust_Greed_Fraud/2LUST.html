LUST is the second layer of hell and is the most advanced of the floors having pretty solid technolagy when it comes to their windy situation. LUST is a giant city with really tall skyscrapers, with a sprinkle of some small nice little homes here and there. The homes and skyscrapers look and act like any other home and skyscraper, usualy.

The statement of being the most advanced does not mean it is more advanced than the city's in our world, it's just very similar. But that shouldn't deter you from going to LUST, it has many nice hotels, museums, swimming pools, gyms and more to go to. There sadly is no park but atleast you also have the whole city to explore! You can learn the history of the LUST layer and how the city was made and/or learn about King Minos as there is alot of history.

And of course the basics the trip needs. LUST obviously has shelter and beds, it has food and water and residents who might be a little too kind. Over all a nice place to be at.

As nice as it sounds there are a few things that could and probably be a bit of a problem. Remember the part when i mentioned ''windy situation''? well i said that mainly because LUST have really strong winds, and are stronger the more the person prioritizes sex, love or anything similar over important things, so do be careful about that. Another thing that you may or may not notice is the ''Corspe of King Minos'' laying dead over a few buildings. I should have probably mentioned that King Minos was a giant and towers over most of the skyscrapers and the reason why he's deadis because Gabriel killed him after heaven found out Minos made a city in hell.

Now as bad as a giant may sound like it isn't as bad as it sounds, the city is really big so you're most likely going to be far from the corspe and the other things i mentioned kinda out weigh the consof LUST. So if you want to go to a cool city without the risk of being hurt then LUST is for you.
